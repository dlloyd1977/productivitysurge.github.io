Effective April 19, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

ProductivitySurge, LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates ProductivitySurge, LLC applications and this website. This page describes our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our products and services.

Website Analytics And Your Choice

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager to understand visits, page views, referrals, downloads, and interactions such as outbound links and scrolling. Google receives technical information associated with these requests, including browser and device information and network information. Analytics cookies distinguish visits. We do not use this setup for advertising personalization.

Website analytics is off until you choose Accept Analytics. You can choose Reject Analytics without affecting the website's content or downloads. Use the Analytics Settings button on any content page to change your choice. Rejecting after acceptance reloads the page to stop measurement and clears accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site; it does not delete previously collected data.

Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page only. Analytics remains disabled when JavaScript is disabled. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

DocReclaimer

1.1 Document Data (Processed Locally)

DocReclaimer analyzes document content (PDF text, filenames, metadata) solely to generate rename suggestions. In self-hosted and desktop mode:

  • All document processing occurs locally on your device.
  • We do not transmit, store, or access the content of your documents.
  • No document data leaves your machine unless you explicitly enable optional third-party AI features.

When analytics are enabled, we collect anonymous aggregate data such as app launches, feature usage counts, and error codes. We do not track file names, file contents, file paths, document text, or personal identifiers through analytics events.

Markdown A.I.

Markdown A.I. is a macOS, iPadOS and Android markdown editor that works with documents and folders you choose. Document content is processed locally on your device unless you explicitly configure the macOS app to use a separate AI endpoint.

Local Processing

  • Editing, preview rendering, file indexing, and iPad Local A.I. inference occur on your device.
  • ProductivitySurge, LLC does not receive or store the contents of your markdown files.
  • The iPad app uses Apple Foundation Models when available and a bundled Qwen model as its on-device fallback. Prompts, document text, responses, and conversation history are not sent to ProductivitySurge, LLC, Apple Private Cloud Compute, the model publisher, or another inference service.
  • Settings, security-scoped file bookmarks, and session state are stored locally on the device. Local A.I. prompts, responses, and conversation history remain memory-only.

User-Selected Files Providers

On iPad, you may grant access to files through Apple's document picker, including iCloud Drive, On My iPad, SMB locations, or attached storage shown by the Files app. Markdown A.I. receives access only to the items you select. Files-provider credentials remain managed by Apple and the selected provider; ProductivitySurge, LLC does not receive them.

Optional macOS Local Agent

If you enable the macOS Local Agent, prompts derived from your current document may be sent to an AI endpoint you configure (for example Ollama or LM Studio on your network). That transmission is initiated by your machine to your chosen endpoint — not to ProductivitySurge, LLC servers.

Analytics

Markdown A.I. does not include third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or cross-app tracking.

App Store Purchases

Apple processes Markdown A.I. subscription purchases, trials, billing, refunds, and payment information. The app receives StoreKit-signed product and entitlement information needed to display localized offers and determine access, such as subscription status, expiration, introductory-offer eligibility, billing-retry or grace-period state, and revocation. ProductivitySurge, LLC does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple handles purchase data under Apple's privacy policy and App Store terms.

Android Documents And Google Play Purchases

The Android edition processes editing, previews and drafts on your device. Files you select through Android document providers remain subject to the selected provider's privacy practices. The Android local artificial-intelligence feature is currently a demonstration and does not send prompts or document content to an inference service.

Google Play processes Android subscription purchases, eligible trials, renewals, cancellations, refunds and payment details. Markdown A.I. uses the Google Play Billing Library to receive product prices, eligible offers, signed purchase information and current subscription ownership, and to acknowledge completed purchases. The app validates purchase signatures locally. This release does not send purchase tokens to a ProductivitySurge verification server and does not persist tokens or a permanent entitlement flag in its own storage. Google Play manages its own billing data under Google's privacy policy and Google Play terms. ProductivitySurge does not receive full payment-card details. Documents, drafts and editor contents are not included in billing requests.
