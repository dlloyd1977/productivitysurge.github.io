Age Suitability

Effective August 2, 2026 · For App Store Connect Age Suitability URL

This page provides age-suitability information for ProductivitySurge applications distributed on the Apple App Store. It is the canonical URL for the App Store Connect Age Suitability URL field.

Summary

  • ProductivitySurge apps are productivity tools for writing, document organization, and storage hygiene.
  • They are rated for a general audience. Current App Store questionnaire results for Markdown A.I. calculate to 4+.
  • They are not “Made for Kids.”
  • They do not include social media capabilities as defined by Apple (no social feed or similar discovery method used to redistribute, amplify, or interact with user-generated content).
  • They are not intended for children under 13. See our Privacy Policy.

Markdown A.I. / Markdown A.I. Plus

Markdown A.I. is a local-first markdown editor with live preview. Optional AI features run on the user’s device or on an endpoint the user configures. The apps do not provide:

  • A social feed, public timeline, or content discovery surface
  • User profiles, follows, likes, or comments on other users’ content
  • Redistribution or amplification of third-party user-generated content
  • Unrestricted public chat with strangers

Users work with documents and folders they choose. Content stays under the user’s control unless they explicitly enable an optional AI endpoint they configure.

App Store age rating: 4+ (questionnaire-derived; not Made for Kids; no social media content descriptor expected).

DocReclaimer

DocReclaimer helps rename and organize documents locally. It processes files the user selects and does not include social feeds, public UGC discovery, or social media interaction features.

Intended audience: general / productivity users. Not Made for Kids. Not intended for children under 13.

Empty Folder Manager

Empty Folder Manager helps find and review empty folders for safe cleanup. It is a local storage-hygiene utility with no social media or user-generated-content feed features.

Intended audience: general / productivity users. Not Made for Kids. Not intended for children under 13.

Parental controls and Time Allowances

Because these apps do not offer social media capabilities, they are not intended to be classified in Apple’s Social Media Time Allowance category based on social-feed features. Parents should still use device-level Screen Time and related controls as appropriate for their household.
